I served my National Service obligations as an Admin Support Assistant (internally designated as an AI Engineer / Researcher) at the [RSAF Agile Innovation Digital (RAiD)](https://www.rsaf.gov.sg/raid/), under their Experimentations branch, [AETHER](https://aether.sutd.edu.sg/) based at SUTD.

My role in AETHER was a diverse one, ranging from administrative workload (managing general operations across manpower, logistics and finance), team management and vision-setting for projects that would exist way beyond my time there. I served as team lead for the Cognitive Superiority and AETHER AI Agents (A3) Teams throughout the 2 years, supporting various capability buildup there.
