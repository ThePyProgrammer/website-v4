arXiv.nush is a full-stack research management and discovery platform designed for NUS High School of Mathematics and Science. It provides a centralized repository for students and teachers to document, explore, and manage research projects, replacing fragmented administrative records with a structured relational database. Students can showcase their research through personalized profiles, project abstracts, reports, publications, and competition achievements, while teachers can track project submissions, mentorship relationships, and research outcomes. The platform also supports research discovery through searchable student, mentor, and project records, alongside statistics highlighting individual research accomplishments. arXiv.nush aims to make institutional research more accessible, organized, and connected across generations of students.
