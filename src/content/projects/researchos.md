ResearchOS is a research management platform combining a Zotero-like reference manager with an semi-agentic research workflow engine. It allows researchers to organize academic papers, websites, GitHub repositories, and experimental notes within a shared knowledge library, with semantic search, citation management, and interactive knowledge graphs. Beyond conventional reference management, ResearchOS integrates AI agents capable of assisting with literature discovery, analysis, and research workflows while maintaining traceability to original sources. ResearchOS is my approach to a unified environment for managing and accelerating scientific research.
